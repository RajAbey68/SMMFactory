- **Terminology Rule:** Always use **"Villa"** or **"Lakeside Villa"**. **NEVER** use "estate", "resort", or "hotel".
- **Website:** [www.kolakevilla.com](https://www.kolakevilla.com)

## 2. Accurate Accommodation & Bedroom Structure
- **Total Bedrooms:** **7 AC en-suite bedrooms**
- **Suites:** **2 Large Family Suites** (each sleeps 5 or 6 people)

## 5. Campaign Ad Qualification & Copywriting Rules
- **Plain English Copywriting Standard:** Use warm, clear, plain English for consumer-facing ads (e.g., *"Rent Entire 7-Bedroom Lakeside Villa"*). Exceptions are accepted industry terms for retreats, surfing, yoga, wellness, corporate offsites, and DMCs (e.g., *"Exclusive Villa Buyout"*, *"Yoga Deck"*, *"Team Workation"*, *"DMC Incentive Travel"*).
- **Target Group Segments:** 1) Corporate Offsites & Team Retreats (Sales, Executive, Partner, Client), 2) Incentive Travel DMCs & Corporate Rewards Agencies, 3) Yoga, Wellness, Tai-Chi & Creative Retreat Facilitators (Arts, Acting, Public Speaking, Writing, Photography), 4) Surf Groups & Basecamps, 5) Family Reunions, Weddings & Milestone Celebrations (Anniversaries, Birthdays).
- **In-Country Language Filter (Sri Lanka Ads):** Exclude local native languages (`Sinhala` & `Tamil`). Target international guest languages (`English`, `Spanish`, `Portuguese`, `Arabic`, `Hebrew`, `French`, `German`, `Russian`, `Italian`, `Mandarin`, `Cantonese`, `Malay`). This blocks local laborers/tuk drivers while welcoming non-English HNW travelers from Israel, Gulf States, Europe, Latin America (Brazil/Mexico/etc.), Spain, Portugal, and SE Asia (Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia).
- **Origin-Market Campaigns (Gulf, Israel, Europe):** Target native languages (`Arabic` in Gulf, `Hebrew` in Israel, `French`/`German`/`English` in EU) combined with **Top 10% Household Income** and **Luxury Travel Behavior**.
- **Hardware Tier Gate:** Mandate **Apple iOS** & **Flagship Androids (Samsung S-series/Pixel)**. Exclude budget handsets.
- **Form Qualification Gate:** Require travel dates and party size selection via **Meta Higher-Intent Instant Form** before WhatsApp opens.
- **Placements:** Exclude Meta Audience Network.
